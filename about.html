Our Structure

Beyond Single-Trade Reselling

Antham Enterprise was established in Howrah, West Bengal, to provide government offices, PSUs, universities, and corporate institutions with a dependable partner capable of orchestrating complex procurement and contract execution.

Traditional suppliers are frequently restricted to single product lines—either printing alone, basic stationery, or localised building trades. When an institutional tender demands coordinated delivery of materials, site logistics, installation, or structural maintenance, indenter departments are forced to coordinate multiple disparate entities.

Antham Enterprise operates under an integrated contracting model:

Requirement Analysis → Sourcing → Pricing → Procurement → Coordination → Delivery & Execution

This workflow ensures that every specification clause, transit deadline, and inspection requirement is managed under one unified point of accountability.
